# GJP HR Web

Reading this as: an operational HR dashboard for daily attendance review, in a firm GJP corporate language, dial ENERGY 2 / RHYTHM 2 / MOTION 1.

- Color: GJP blue is the working color because it owns the wordmark; GJP red is reserved for urgent or destructive actions.
- Layout: a horizontal masthead keeps the logo visible and gives operational data the full page width.
- Typography: Aptos and Segoe UI feel administrative and familiar, with tabular numerals for times and counts.
- Spacing: dense tables use a 6px base rhythm while page sections receive larger pauses to separate decisions.
- Surfaces: most content stays flat; only the daily attendance focal panel receives elevation.
- Theme: fixed light surfaces suit document-heavy HR review and preserve the supplied logo's intended contrast.
- Motif: the blue-red split rule repeats the proportions and hard edges of the GJP wordmark.
- Motion: hover and focus transitions only, because HR review should remain calm.
- Enrollment: foto acuan melekat pada baris identitas karyawan, sedangkan daftar perangkat sengaja bersifat observasional tanpa aksi media.
